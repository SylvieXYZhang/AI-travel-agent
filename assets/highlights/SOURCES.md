Source URL format: `https://images.unsplash.com/photo-{image-id}`.

The destination-level photographs are bound only to broad, accurately named overview cards (for example, "阿那亚海滩" or "多瑙河两岸"). They are never reused for a specific landmark card unless the pictured landmark is verified. Specific landmark cards without a verified static asset retain the retrieval/generation fallback state.
